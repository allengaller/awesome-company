# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Open-source contributors and developers who curate or consume "awesome-lists"; they skim fast, judge credibility in seconds, and star/share what feels authoritative.
- People exploring the global tech-company landscape (students, recruiters, founders, engineers) who need trustworthy official entry points — sites, tech blogs, GitHub orgs, careers pages — without SEO noise.
- Context: mostly desktop browsers, often from GitHub or social shares; bilingual Chinese/English readers.

## Product Purpose

Awesome Company is a curated navigation list of 500+ global technology companies across four sectors (互联网 / 软件 / 硬件 / 周边上下游), each entry linking official sites, products, tech blogs, developer communities, GitHub orgs, and careers pages. The GTM page's job: convince a first-time visitor in under a minute that this is the authoritative index of the tech industry, then convert them into a star, a contributor (new company entries), or a user of the list. Success = stars, PRs, shares, and return visits to the README.

## Brand Personality

全面 · 严谨 · 可信 — exhaustive, rigorous, trustworthy. The voice of a well-maintained atlas or industry registry: precise counts, structured taxonomy, no hype. Quiet confidence carried by density and order, not slogans.

## Anti-references

- Magazine editorial lane: italic display serifs, drop caps, broadsheet grids, cream/paper backgrounds.
- Generic SaaS template: blue-purple gradients, gradient text, glassmorphism cards, identical icon+heading+text card grids, big-number hero-metric clichés.
- Neon/cyberpunk dark-tech maximalism.

## Design Principles

1. **Density is the pitch.** The catalog itself is the hero; show real taxonomy and scale instead of marketing adjectives.
2. **Count what is real.** Every number on the page must be verifiable from the README; no inflated metrics.
3. **Bilingual as one voice.** Chinese carries the narrative; English rides alongside as structural labels — never two parallel walls of text.
4. **Order as aesthetics.** Rhythm comes from structure, hierarchy, and restraint — not decoration.
5. **Prove it, don't promise it.** Show an actual company entry and the contribution workflow rather than claiming quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks for every animation.
- Keyboard-navigable anchors and controls; semantic landmark structure.
